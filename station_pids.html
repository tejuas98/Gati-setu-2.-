What is PIDS?

Mounted on railway station concourses and platform overbridges across all 10,083 stations, the Passenger Information Display System (PIDS) fulfills Problem Statement 26028 by providing dual-clock arrival transparency: it compares the static timetable Scheduled Arrival (STA) against GatiSetu's physics-informed Dynamic Expected Arrival (ETA), assigns real platform berths, and broadcasts automated 15-minute platform starvation/conflict alerts.
