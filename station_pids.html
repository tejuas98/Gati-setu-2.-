What is PIDS?

Dual-Clock Concourse Architecture

Mounted on railway station concourses and platform overbridges across all 10,083 Indian Railways stations, the Passenger Information Display System (PIDS) fulfills Problem Statement 26028 by providing dual-clock arrival transparency:

1. Static Scheduled Arrival (STA) Official timetable nominal arrival time published by Indian Railways.
2. Dynamic Expected Arrival (ETA) GatiSetu's physics-informed AI forecast factoring section gradients, train resistance, and corridor headways.
Automated Platform Conflict Guard: Evaluates track circuit occupancy and platform starvation 15 minutes before arrival. When two trains converge on the same platform berth simultaneously, an automated red alert is triggered for the Section Controller.
